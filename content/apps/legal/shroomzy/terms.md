## 18. Mushroom Identification and Safety Disclaimer

Shroomzy may provide AI-generated information about mushroom identification, species, scientific classification, habitat, seasonality, appearance, toxicity, edibility, look-alikes, and similar matches.

This information is provided for general educational and informational purposes only.

Mushroom identification through photographs is inherently uncertain. Many edible mushrooms closely resemble toxic or deadly species. Results may be affected by image quality, lighting, angle, missing parts of the mushroom, geographic location, seasonal variation, and similarities between species.

We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, safety, or reliability of any identification or mushroom-related information.

You must not eat, taste, prepare, handle, sell, or otherwise use a mushroom based solely on information provided by Shroomzy.

Users should consult a qualified local mycologist or mushroom expert before consuming or using any wild mushroom.

If mushroom poisoning or exposure is suspected, contact local emergency services or a poison control centre immediately. Shroomzy does not provide medical, toxicological, emergency, or treatment advice.
