## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Shroomzy: Mushroom Identifier ("Shroomzy," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Shroomzy mobile application and related services.

Shroomzy provides AI-powered mushroom identification, mushroom information, collections, community features, AI Chat, and other mushroom-related tools. The app may include functionality similar to Trackzio’s other identification apps, including photo identification, personal collections, global catalogues, AI Chat, community posts, subscriptions, and account management.

## 5. Important Mushroom Safety Disclaimer

Mushroom identification through photographs is inherently uncertain. Shroomzy may produce incorrect, incomplete, or misleading results.

Information about:

- Edibility
- Toxicity
- Poisoning risk
- Look-alike species
- Medicinal use
- Preparation
- Habitat
- Identification characteristics

is provided for general educational and informational purposes only.

Do not eat, taste, handle, prepare, sell, or use a mushroom based solely on an identification or statement provided by Shroomzy.

Always obtain confirmation from a qualified local mycologist, mushroom expert, poison-control authority, or other appropriate professional before consuming or using any wild mushroom.

If mushroom poisoning or exposure is suspected, contact local emergency services or a poison-control centre immediately. Do not rely on Shroomzy for emergency, medical, toxicological, or treatment advice.
